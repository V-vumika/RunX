# Code of Conduct

RunX is a small, friendly project. Contributors and users are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Assume good faith — most disagreements are misunderstandings, not conflicts.
- Avoid harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be raised via a GitHub issue, or directly
to a maintainer listed in `AUTHORS.md`. Maintainers will remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, and issues that don't follow this standard, and may
ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

This is a short, practical policy rather than a legal document — the goal is
simply a welcoming project for people learning DSA and complexity analysis.
